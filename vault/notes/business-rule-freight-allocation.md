# Freight is allocated by weight

## Statement
Freight costs are allocated to products proportionally by weight, not by value or unit count.

## Rationale
The factory's freight contracts charge by kg shipped. Allocating by value would over-weight high-value low-weight products.

## Applies to
- All cost center actuals datasets
- Monthly freight variance reports

## Exceptions
- Rush/expedited shipments use actual cost, not weight allocation
- Inter-plant transfers at cost, no freight allocation
