# Cost Variance

## Definition
The difference between actual cost and budget/standard cost for a given period and cost center.

## Formula
`Cost Variance = Actual Amount - Budget Amount`

Negative = favorable (spent less than budget)
Positive = unfavorable (spent more than budget)

## Source
Tool: `T-PVM-001` (price_volume_mix)
Tables: `clean_actuals` joined with `budget`

## Frequency
Monthly, per cost center, per material group
