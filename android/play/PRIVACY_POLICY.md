# Privacy Policy - MM Core (Android app)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The app "MM Core" shows the dashboard of the company's own MM Core ERP system on the phone.

**What the app does**
- It connects only to the MM Core PC of your company in your local Wi-Fi, which you choose by
  scanning a QR code shown on that PC.
- It displays the dashboard figures of that PC (sales, finance, production, stock, HR summary).
  It cannot change any data.

**Data we collect: none.**
- The app does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
- The only data stored on the phone is the address of your MM Core PC and the pairing key
  (a cookie), so that the dashboard opens directly next time. Uninstalling the app removes them.

**Camera**
- To scan the QR code the app uses the Google Play services code scanner. The app itself does
  not get access to the camera or to images; it only receives the text of the scanned code.

**Security**
- The dashboard data travels only inside your local network between the phone and your MM Core PC.
- The pairing key can be renewed on the PC at any time ("New key"), which disconnects every phone.

**Contact**
- For questions about this policy contact the publisher of the app (see the Google Play listing).
